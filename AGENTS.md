# Product intent

Astra and other engineering agents: read this file, then read [CAPABILITIES.md](CAPABILITIES.md) for the complete A–L creative scope, the cross-domain quality standard, and the independently measurable acceptance gates. It is a product contract, not a model prompt or a claim of implementation.

Build one coherent creative system that an AI model can operate as an artist
operates professional creative software. The AI supplies ideas and creative
decisions; BODY supplies the working capabilities, project state, feedback and
delivery. Specialist engines work together as parts of one system.

The intended breadth covers graphic and thumbnail design, photo and vector
editing, typography, video editing, motion design, 2D/3D animation, geometry,
materials, cameras, lighting, CGI, simulation, VFX, compositing, color and sound.
Advertisements, films and educational videos are outputs assembled from these
capabilities; they do not define a fixed project structure or visual style.

# Control and workflow

Give the AI detailed native project control: assets, clips, tracks, layers,
objects, text, nodes, masks, keyframes, curves, timing, speed, effects, color,
camera, light, audio and render settings. It must be able to inspect the actual
state, see/hear results, revise selected parts, preserve versions and export.
Art direction, duration, composition and technique belong to each project.

Use mature, powerful open-source engines where they fit. Integrate their useful
native capabilities into the common project workflow. Installing an engine or
exposing a small subset of it does not complete a creative field.

# Development direction

The owner's latest instruction is to adopt complete capable open-source
applications rather than rebuild their features one primitive at a time.
Source research informs the choice; integration then completes one field before
expanding. The earlier research-only freeze is superseded. Implementation,
architectural decisions and repository updates are authorized. Preserve useful
working code, originals and history. The full product scope remains in
[CAPABILITIES.md](CAPABILITIES.md).

Current engineering choices, source-backed comparisons and unchecked research
gates are preserved in [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md),
[docs/reuse-research.md](docs/reuse-research.md) and
[docs/video-research-plan.md](docs/video-research-plan.md). Read those before
repeating experiments or proposing a rewrite. They are design/evidence records,
not new product requirements or a claim that research gates have passed.

During application reuse and integration:

Complete one creative field to a reliable professional production standard before
expanding into the next. The current focus is the complete pinned Velorn editor
and its standard MCP connection to a replaceable local brain. Finish native
editing, timing, sound, color, custody and recovery together; preserve useful
source-aware MLT/OTIO routes and the working Blender motion system.
Evaluate practical capability, control, output quality and reliability against
leading professional workflows. Treat exceptional quality as a target requiring
evidence, rather than an already achieved ranking.

Inspect the current source and actual installed capabilities before changing
anything. Preserve working behavior and project data. Verify relevant native
outputs, revisions, dependencies, failure/recovery and representative workloads.
Distinguish implementation, technical tests and artistic acceptance. Use an
actual connected AI model when evaluating autonomous operation; a scripted test
does not establish that result.

Keep public documentation concise and useful. Do not add conversation transcripts,
progress diaries, repeated status files or dated handoff documents. Keep essential
usage instructions, licenses, schemas and executable tests accurate.
