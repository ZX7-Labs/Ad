## THE GOAL IS NOT A TOY VIDEO GENERATOR

Build an autonomous, no-mandatory-paid-cost, general-purpose **world-class AI creative filmmaker and editor** able to take an ordinary Hindi/English phone conversation and independently conceive, research, direct, design, compose, light, animate, edit, produce and revise short-form/long-form films, advertisements, documentary, educational videos, motion graphics, genuine 3D and visual-effects-heavy works. Target aesthetic excellence: a viewer says "wow" because its imagery, decisions, rhythm and originality work as a coherent film. Eventually surpass what even excellent human editors can conceive, if actual independent audience tests demonstrate it. This is a **research goal**, not a proven present-day claim.

The Director must **think like an outstanding independent artist**: imagine first; use the best rights-cleared, genuinely executable tools appropriate to the idea; select craft choices, medium, genre, camera, story, format, voice and pace for the individual commission; see its own results; make principled revisions. Do not replace imagination with generic cards or fixed effects. The user delegates model choice, tool choice, research, implementation and testing to the assistant and expressly does not want repeated explanations/permission to choose technical approaches.

**Do not hardcode creative decisions.** No universal scene number, genre, duration, format, color, font, animation style, effect list, narration, shot template, presumed product photo, default 2D/2.5D, specific editor model or fixed renderer. Typed contracts may enforce factual provenance, media rights, tool availability, security and product quality; those are NOT artistic formulas. A renderer's local limits do not limit the film vision; unsupported media should remain unresolved or be acquired legally, not silently replaced by a flat slide.

**Only one cohesive creative field intelligence**, trained/evaluated for filmmaking, directing, editing, storytelling, design and audiovisual taste rather than a general-purpose coding agent or a herd of loaded specialist LLMs. General supporting render/audio/video tools are necessary, not independent artist personas. Film-trained multimodal research and factual evidence are more important than JSON compliance. A 3B model winning camera-shot labeling is not proof it can invent extraordinary cinema. No model should become the permanent Director until *actual diverse films* justify it.

**Free/open/rights-verified first.** Use a public GitHub Actions free CPU runner while eligible, local models (llama.cpp), licensed Blender, FFmpeg, compositor, pinned Supertonic M3/F2 voices. Open-source packages may indirectly depend on expensive AI APIs, GPU or restricted weights; no unattended payment or surprise paid Jobs. Content and licenses are checked separately. Existing historical projects and samples are NEVER overwritten; stable `main` stays protected until genuinely accepted.
