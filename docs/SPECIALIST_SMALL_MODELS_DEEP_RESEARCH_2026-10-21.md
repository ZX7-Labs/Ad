## Small domain models with meaningful, narrowly demonstrated advantages

1. **ShotVL-3B**, official weights https://huggingface.co/Vchitect/ShotVL-3B ; Apache-2.0 weights, Qwen2.5-VL-3B-based. Cinematography image/video interpretation from ShotQA-70k. Reported original ShotBench accuracy: ShotVL-3B 65.1%; Qwen2.5-VL-72B 59.1%; GPT-4o 59.3%. Primary source: https://github.com/Vchitect/ShotBench . **Important independent falsification:** https://github.com/wuhang03/RefineShot found original benchmark ambiguity and ShotVL instruction-following/reasoning inconsistency. Conclusion: candidate film-language **observer**, NOT autonomous idea writer or world-class creative authority. Must re-test actual film previews on ZX7 and measure output conformity. No GPU inference done yet.
2. **Writer-R1-4B** creative writing model described in https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15061 (Mar 2026). Paper claims strong creative-writing improvements and beats selected 100B+ open-weight models on its writing evaluations. It is **not filmmaker-trained**, and currently **no official verified open model checkpoint/code/license found** via Hugging Face/GitHub search. Do **not** install or mark downloadable; method of dynamic criteria + memory-augmented replay gives a promising film-specific future adaptation idea, not an earned score in ZX7.
3. **Q-ReAlign Mini 0.8B**, official weights https://huggingface.co/q-future/Q-ReAlign-Mini-0.8B ; Apache-2.0. Dedicated image/video subjective perceptual quality and aesthetics scorer. Authors report avg SRCC 0.879 vs older Q-Align 0.869 across 7 quality benchmarks: https://github.com/Q-Future/Q-ReAlign . Runs on CPU (slow) / usually <4GB GPU memory. NOT narrative creativity or shot relevance. Useful *only after* decoding real frames.
4. **AutoCut** ad editing, https://adautocut.github.io/ and https://github.com/AdAutoCut/Autocut ; checkpoint https://huggingface.co/MiltonZ/AutoCut_embsft labelled Apache-2.0 weights, ~8B Qwen3 backbone with independent video/audio encoders. CVPR 2026 reports clip ranking 0.107 vs GPT-4o+MGSV 0.078 and music similarity 0.348 vs 0.266. This is EDITING REASONING not source-shot generation, and requires a prepared clip collection and more resource than current CPU director. Source dataset URLs explicitly **restrict commercial reuse without rights**; do not assume whole released bundle is commercially cleared. No ready lightweight local adapter verified.
5. **Kiwi-Edit 5B**, https://github.com/showlab/Kiwi-Edit ; open MIT code, published pretrained variants at https://huggingface.co/linyq/kiwi-edit-5b-instruct-reference-diffusers . Trained for instruction/reference-based physical **video-to-video pixel modifications** such as replace/remove/add subject or background, with visual consistency. Real source, code and weights exist. The installation docs specify CUDA 12.8 and GPU, and jointly rely on multi-GB Qwen-VL/video backbone/VAE beyond nominal 5B DiT; NOT a free GitHub CPU editor, film writer or instant all-in-one. Check weight/base licenses before any deployment.
6. **OmniScript 8B**, https://github.com/TencentARC/OmniScript ; trained to convert already recorded cinematic video into hierarchical scripts, and authors report comparison against large VLMs. **Weights currently under internal open-source review, not released** at the time of checked README. Cannot call a candidate executable.
7. **CinematicVQA**, https://research.google/pubs/cinematicvqa-benchmarking-film-grammar-reasoning-in-large-vision-language-models/ (Sept 2026): valuable evidence that visual recognition is not knowing narrative **function** and audience effect. Its train data may aid future *rights-cleared* cinematic reasoning evaluation/training. No verified small end-to-end downloadable model in this research.

## What is NOT established

- No single public 0.8–5B checkpoint has been shown to beat all strong film editors at narrative conception + design + actual 3D + sound + timeline editing + VFX, judged on *real completed movies*. One domain score is not an all-domain claim.
- A 3B specialist can beat a 72B general model on *one* benchmark and still miss instructions or lack originality. Domain-specific comparative gains are possible, not magical universal creative superiority.
- No public free service has verified entitlement to run arbitrary always-on GPU editing at zero expense. All GPU offerings need availability, privacy, rights and $0 balance before invocation.
- Source movie clips/benchmark data may be copyrighted even if model weights/code have permissive licenses. No commercial training or redistribution just because a public link exists.
